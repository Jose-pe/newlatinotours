Manu 4 Days Experience

Immerse Yourself in the Heart of the Amazon Rainforest

Duration 4 Days / 3 Nights
Location Manu National Park
Group Size 5 - 10 people
Difficulty Easy - Moderate

Manu Rainforest Experience with Locals

Uncover the Amazon’s mysteries and legends on this 4-day expedition into Manu National Park, a vast Amazonian Biosphere covering nearly 20,000 sq km. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Manu is home to unmatched biodiversity: pristine primary rainforests, oxbow lakes, parrot clay licks, giant kapok trees, and native indigenous communities. Experience wild Amazonian nature guided by local experts!

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1
Cusco – Cloud Forest – Pona Lodge

Early 4:30 AM pickup from your hotel. Visit the pre-Inca funeral towers of Ninamarca and colonial Paucartambo before reaching Acjanaco Pass (4,000m), the entrance to Manu National Park. Descend through lush cloud forest filled with waterfalls, orchids, capuchin monkeys, and exotic birds. Arrive at Pona Lodge for dinner and overnight rest.

Day 2
Pona Lodge – Machuwasi Lagoon – Robles Treehouse Lodge

Explore local fruit orchards and coca plantations before driving to Atalaya port. Board a motorized riverboat along the Alto Madre de Dios River to trek to Machuwasi Lagoon. Spot prehistoric hoatzin birds, screamers, and caimans on balsa rafts. Continue upstream to Robles Treehouse Lodge for an eco-stay in stilted treehouses and optional nocturnal wildlife walk.

Day 3
Robles Treehouse Lodge – Parrot Clay Lick – Manu Rainforest Lodge

Early morning visit to the clay licks where hundreds of macaws and parrots gather along the riverbank walls. Guided jungle trek to view giant ancient Ficus and Ceiba trees while observing rainforest insects, frogs, and monkeys. Take a boat ride upstream to Manu Rainforest Lodge for dinner and evening relaxation.

Day 4
Manu Rainforest Lodge – Cusco

Early morning river boat ride back to Atalaya port, watching capybaras and tropical birds along the riverbanks. Board our private minivan for the return drive up through the cloud forest, stopping for lunch along a scenic mountain valley. Arrive back at your Cusco hotel around 6:00 PM.
