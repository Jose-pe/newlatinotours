Traditional Celebrations of Cusco

June 24th
Inti Raymi

Each year on June 24th in the esplanade of Sacsayhuaman, the traditional Inca festivity of Inti Raymi (Festival of the Sun) is celebrated with ancestral rituals and colorful reenactments.

June (Movable)
Corpus Christi

A movable Catholic festivity in June and one of the most colorful, solemn processions celebrated in Cusco, where 15 patron saints are carried through the city center.

July 15th - 18th
Paucartambo - Tres Cruces

Festival of the Virgen del Carmen featuring religious ceremonies, masked traditional dances, and overnight trips to watch the spectacular sunrise at Tres Cruces viewpoint over the Amazon.

May / June
Qoylloriti

A unique movable feast combining Andean indigenous traditions and Catholic faith: a massive pilgrimage to the sanctuary of Our Lord of Qoylloriti at the foot of Sinakara mountain.
