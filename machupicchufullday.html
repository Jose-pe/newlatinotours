Machu Picchu Full Day by Train

We invite you to discover the sacred Incan sanctuary of Machu Picchu with us! Recharge your spiritual energy in the magical lost city of the Incas while enjoying a seamless, fully organized roundtrip excursion from Cusco.

This full-day tour is tailored for travelers who want to experience the magic of Machu Picchu comfortably in one day, enjoying scenic train travel through the Sacred Valley and comprehensive guided exploration.

Sit back and relax as we handle all logistical details including private transfers, train passes, bus tickets, and official entrance permits.

Detailed Itinerary

Scenic Transfer to Ollantaytambo: Early in the morning, we pick you up from your Cusco hotel and drive through the Sacred Valley to the Ollantaytambo train station.

Train Ride to Aguas Calientes: Board the train winding along the Urubamba River through lush cloud forest scenery until reaching Aguas Calientes (Machu Picchu Pueblo).

Guided Citadel Tour: Board the shuttle bus up to the archaeological sanctuary of Machu Picchu (25-min ride). Join your private expert guide to explore the temples, agricultural terraces, and iconic vistas of the citadel.

Return to Cusco: In the afternoon, return by bus to Aguas Calientes, take the train back to Ollantaytambo, and transfer in private transport back to your hotel in Cusco.

What's Included
  • Pre-tour briefing one day before departure
  • Customized roundtrip transfers (Cusco - Ollantaytambo Station)
  • Roundtrip train tickets (Ollantaytambo - Aguas Calientes)
  • Roundtrip shuttle bus tickets (Aguas Calientes - Machu Picchu)
  • Official entrance permit to Machu Picchu Citadel
  • Professional licensed tour guide (English / Spanish)
Recommendations
  • Original passport or ID
  • Arrive 30 mins early at station
  • Max 7kg luggage on train
  • Small daypack (camera, water)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sun hat & sunglasses
  • Sunscreen & insect repellent
  • Rain jacket / poncho
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Visit thermal baths in Aguas Calientes
