Machu Picchu Full Day by train

We invite you to get to know Machu Picchu with us! In this “1 Day Tour Cusco Machu Picchu” recharge your energy in the magical city of the Incas and just enjoy, as we will have everything arranged for you.

Detailed Itinerary

Very early in the morning we will pick you from your hotel in Cusco to the train station, to start your Machu Picchu trip for 1 day. In Ollantaytambo we will board the train that passes through the Sacred Valley of the Incas until we reach the town of Aguas Calientes.

We will pick you from the train station in Aguas Calientes to take you to the bus station, we will take the bus that will take you to the Archaeological Sanctuary of Machu Picchu. The trip lasts 25 minutes, we will finally arrive in Machu Picchu where you will recharge your energies and also enjoy the breathtaking view from the top of the mountain where our guide will begin the guided tour so you can know better the history and architecture of the Inca Citadel of Machu Picchu.

Visiting Machu Picchu. In the afternoon we will board the train back to Ollantaytambo, where we will pick you up from the train station to transfer you to your hotel in Cusco.This is how you end your Machu Picchu 1 day tour.

  • Included:
  • Briefing one day before tour.
  • Customized transfers from Cusco to the train station in Ollantaytambo, round trip.
  • Train tickets Ollantaytambo – Aguas Calientes, round trip.
  • Bust tickets Aguas Calientes – Machu Picchu, round trip.
  • Entrance to Machu Picchu.
  • Professional guide English / Spanish (Ask for other languages with additional prices).
  • Recommendations:
  • Carry your identification card or passport.
  • Arrive half an hour before to the train station.
  • Do not carry a lot of luggage: only 7kg are allowed on the train.
  • We suggest carrying a small backpack with your camera, wearing a hat, sunscreen, mosquito repellant and a bringing a bottle of water.
  • In Machu Picchu you will climb many steps, so wear shoes suitable for walking.
  • After the guided tour in Machu Picchu, enjoy the magical moment, open your arms and recharge your energy.
  • Visit the thermal baths
