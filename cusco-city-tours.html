Cusco City Tour

Discover the Ancient Capital of the Inca Empire

Duration Half Day
Location Cusco City & Ruins
Group Size 5 - 10 people
Difficulty Easy

Cusco Traditional Tours

If you spend any time at all in Cusco during your personalized Peru trip, explore the best of Cusco and its surroundings in just half a day. Visiting these beautiful nearby attractions is a fantastic addition to your itinerary.

For your first day in this majestic city, we recommend visiting four spectacular Inca Ruins set just above Cusco City and admiring the surrounding landscape! Cusco maintains its customs, traditions, mysteries, and tales about a legendary past.

Along your journey, you will see local Cusqueñan women dressed in traditional skirts and bowler hats in local markets or near the Plaza de Armas. Come and be one of the first adventurers to make this tour with us!

Detailed Itinerary

Schedule: Daily runs either in the morning (08:00 AM – 01:00 PM) or afternoon (01:00 PM – 06:00 PM).

Q'oricancha: We begin picking you up at your hotel to head to the Temple of the Sun (Q'oricancha), the most important spiritual center of the Incas, where Santo Domingo Church now stands.

Sacsayhuaman: Drive 25 minutes to the outskirts to visit Sacsayhuaman with its massive 200-ton monoliths and spectacular views of the city.

Q'enqo, Puca Pucara & Tambomachay: Explore Q'enqo's labyrinth stone carvings, the fortress shelter of Puca Pucara, and ending at Tambomachay, the Inca Temple of Water. Afterwards, we drop you off at your hotel.

What's Included
  • Pre-departure briefing: Hotel orientation at 5:00 PM the evening before the tour.
  • Professional Guide: Licensed tourism degree graduates fluent in English/Spanish.
  • Transportation: Full round-trip pick-up and drop-off from/to your hotel.
Recommendations
  • Original passport
  • Tourist ticket (Boleto Turístico)
  • Small daypack
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Water bottle
  • Sun hat & sunglasses
  • Sunscreen (SPF 35+)
  • Camera with extra battery
