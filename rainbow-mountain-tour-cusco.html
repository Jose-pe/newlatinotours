Rainbow Mountain Tour

Full Day Hike to Vinicunca (Montaña de Siete Colores)

Duration Full Day
Location Cusco, Llaqto, Vinicunca
Group Size 5 - 10 people
Difficulty Easy to Moderate

The Rainbow Mountain Experience

In this full-day program, we visit one of the most famous natural wonders of Cusco and Peru: The Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca).

The tour begins early in the morning with hotel pickup between 04:00 AM and 04:30 AM. We travel approximately two hours south toward the Cusipata – Paucarpata district, where you will enjoy a delicious warm breakfast before embarking on the trek into the High Andes.

Detailed Itinerary

Trek to Vinicunca: After breakfast, a 1-hour drive brings us to the control point at Llaqto to start our hike. The trek takes approximately 1 hour and 20 minutes across classic Andean landscapes dotted with herds of alpacas and llamas.

Vinicunca Viewpoint (5,080m): Upon reaching the summit viewpoint of Montaña de Colores, your guide will explain the unique geology, mineral composition, and ecosystem of the area. Take ample time for photos and admiring the surrounding Ausangate peak views.

Return to Cusco: We return along the same path to Llaqto, where our private transport takes us back to Cusipata for a well-earned buffet lunch. We return to Cusco by 4:00 PM – 5:00 PM, finishing near the Plaza de Armas.

What's Included
  • Informative briefing prior to tour
  • Round-trip tourist transportation from Cusco
  • Warm blankets inside the vehicle & trekking poles
  • Professional bilingual guide (English - Spanish)
  • Buffet breakfast in Cusipata
  • Buffet lunch in Cusipata
Recommendations
  • Warm thermal clothing & layers
  • Sunscreen (SPF 90+ recommended)
  • UV-protecting sunglasses
  • Warm hat / cap & gloves
  • Trekking poles / walking sticks
  • Rain jacket or poncho
  • Sturdy hiking boots/shoes
  • Personal water bottle
