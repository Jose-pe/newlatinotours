The Sacred Valley of the Incas

All those who are fascinated by the mysterious history and legacy of the Inca Empire can explore the Sacred Valley, nestled in the heart of the Andes mountain range.

The Sacred Valley snakes along a narrow fertile corridor following the Urubamba River between Pisac and Ollantaytambo. It boasts marvelous agricultural landscapes where native Quechua families preserve high-mountain customs and ancestral rites.

The towns exhibit a unique fusion of Incan and Spanish colonial architecture. The rich heritage of its people—expressed in folklore, colorful textiles, and everyday culture—creates unforgettable memories for every traveler.

Detailed Itinerary

Pisac: Visit the world-famous Pisac market, where indigenous Quechua artisans display handcrafted goods and vibrant textiles. Above the town, explore the magnificent Pisac ruins, perched high on mountain ridges with panoramic views over ancient terracing, the Temple of the Sun, and ceremonial fountains.

Ollantaytambo: Tour the imposing Incan fortress built with giant stone monoliths, temples, and agricultural terraces. This strategic site was one of the few places where Spanish forces were defeated in battle during the conquest.

Chinchero: Originally chosen as the royal estate of Inca Tupac Yupanqui, Chinchero features a colonial church constructed directly atop Incan stone foundations, symbolising a unique historical synthesis. End the visit by observing traditional textile weaving demonstrations.
